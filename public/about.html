About Bharat Bhojan

A curated educational information system demonstrating relational database design and web development applied to traditional Indian food heritage.

Section 1

🍲 1. About Bharat Bhojan

Bharat Bhojan (Traditional Indian Food Knowledge Information System) is an educational, database-driven web application developed for BCA 5th-Semester Academic Project #25. It provides an organized, searchable digital repository documenting traditional Indian regional foods, native ingredients, seasonal (Ritu) adaptations, traditional cooking techniques, and cultural context.

Unlike generic recipe blogs that focus solely on rapid commercial instructions, Bharat Bhojan documents food as an integral element of India’s cultural and agro-climatic heritage. It bridges traditional knowledge with computer science, demonstrating how normalized relational databases make complex cultural data easy to explore, cross-reference, and preserve.

Section 2

⚠️ 2. Project Problem

Traditional Indian food knowledge is vast and geographically nuanced, yet it currently faces two major digital problems:

  • Fragmentation & Oral Loss: Ancient cooking practices, seasonal dietary rhythms (Ritu-Charya), and indigenous ingredients (such as sattu, bathua, kokum, and kolakhar) are scattered across regional monographs, regional languages, and oral memories without centralized digital structuring.
  • Lack of Structured Database Modeling: Most existing food websites treat recipes as unindexed plain text blocks. They lack relational categorization by agro-climatic region, seasonality, primary agricultural produce, and verified historical references.

Bharat Bhojan addresses this gap by creating a unified relational schema where foods, states, regions, seasons, ingredients, and scholarly citations are systematically interconnected.

Section 3

🎯 3. Project Objectives

The principal goals of the Bharat Bhojan project are:

  • Cultural Preservation: Digitally archive verified traditional Indian foods with their native scripts, state origins, preparation methods, and historical significance.
  • Database Demonstration: Design, normalize (3NF), and operate a relational database linking foods with states, zones, seasons, ingredients, and references.
  • Multi-Criteria Search & Filtering: Provide instant query capabilities across food names, ingredients, states, seasons, and dietary categories.
  • Interactive Web Portal: Develop a responsive, user-friendly frontend using web fundamentals (HTML5, CSS3, Vanilla JS).
  • Administrative Data Management: Provide an authenticated administrative dashboard allowing authorized users to create, update, and manage food records (CRUD).
  • Academic Transparency: Ground all entries in verifiable bibliographic citations from government gazettes, university press books, and academic journals.
Section 4

📜 4. Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS) Connection

In the context of the National Education Policy and academic research, Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS) encompasses centuries of empirical, experiential wisdom developed across Indian communities. In culinary science, this includes:

🌾 Ritu-Charya (Seasonal Harmony)

Aligning dietary habits with the six natural Indian seasons (Shad-Ritu) and regional crop harvest calendars.

🧪 Indigenous Chemistry

Natural wild lactic fermentation (dosa, dhokla), banana-ash alkali filtration (kolakhar), and slow earthen baking.

🌱 Agro-Biodiversity

Empirical reliance on climate-hardy indigenous grains (sattu, millets, local rice) and seasonal foraged greens (bathua, sarson).

🪔 Sacred & Ritual Nutrition

Ceremonial offerings and community culinary gatherings (prasad, Chhath Thekua, Puri Dalma, Onam Sadya Avial).

Section 5

✨ 5. Main System Features

  • Real-Time Keyword Search: Instant full-text search matching dish names, native scripts, key ingredients, origin states, and cultural narratives.
  • Multi-Criteria Filtering: Combine filters across Geographical Zone, State of Provenance, Season (Ritu), Category, and Dietary Classification simultaneously.
  • Detailed Food Dossiers: Structured presentation of ingredient lists with quantity notes, step-by-step preparation traditions, seasonality context, serving customs, and related dishes.
  • Geographical Zone Navigation: Explore dishes across all 6 zones of India with state breakdowns and culinary hallmarks.
  • Agro-Climatic Seasonal Rhythm: Filter and understand dishes harmonized with Summer, Monsoon, Autumn, Winter, Spring, and Year-round cycles.
  • Bibliographic References Library: Direct links to government gazettes, GI registries, and Oxford academic publications.
  • Session-Protected Admin Dashboard: Secure login with bcrypt password hashing, dynamic metric cards, and full CRUD operations.
Section 6

⚙️ 6. Technology Stack & Architecture

Bharat Bhojan is deliberately constructed using core web development fundamentals to ensure clarity and explainability during academic evaluation:

Frontend Layer

Semantic HTML5, Responsive CSS3 (Custom Properties & Flex/Grid), Vanilla ES6 JavaScript (No React or Tailwind abstraction)

Backend Application

Node.js with Express.js REST API (Model-View-Controller architectural pattern)

Database Management

SQLite via Node.js native engine (node:sqlite DatabaseSync), PRAGMA foreign keys enforced

Security & Sessions

bcryptjs for password hashing, express-session with HttpOnly secure cookie flags

Section 7

🗄️ 7. Database Role & Relational Design

The database is the foundational core of Bharat Bhojan. Rather than storing flat unstructured files, the system employs a normalized relational schema (Third Normal Form - 3NF):

  • Entity Normalization: Primary entities—foods, states, regions, categories, seasons, ingredients, and references_tbl—are stored in dedicated normalized tables with surrogate primary keys.
  • Many-to-Many Relationships: Junction tables (food_ingredients, food_seasons, food_references) manage complex associations with cascade delete constraints (ON DELETE CASCADE).
  • Referential Integrity: SQLite foreign key enforcement (PRAGMA foreign_keys = ON;) ensures orphaned child records cannot exist.
  • Optimized Performance: Explicit B-tree indexes on foreign keys, state names, food slugs, and search fields ensure fast sub-millisecond query responses.
Section 8

🎓 8. Academic Scope

This project is designed as a focused academic prototype for the BCA 5th-Semester curriculum. It demonstrates practical competency in database design, backend API development, data validation, frontend interactivity, security, and software documentation.

The scope encompasses 37 verified regional culinary records chosen across 25 Indian states and union territories, providing an authoritative representation of all 6 geographical zones. Special depth is dedicated to the ancient culinary traditions of Bihar (8 dishes across Magadh, Mithila, Bhojpur, and Anga regions).

Section 9

⚖️ 9. Limitations & Project Disclaimers

Academic Positioning & Transparent Disclaimers:
  • Curated Educational Prototype: The portal documents a curated selection of 37 representative foods. It does not claim to be an exhaustive encyclopedia of every Indian village or community dish.
  • Regional Classifications Overlap: Indian geographical boundaries are culturally porous. Dishes like Khaja or Sattu are enjoyed across Bihar, Bengal, UP, and Odisha with subtle local variations.
  • Household & Community Variations: Traditional preparation methods, spice ratios, and accompaniments vary from home to home. Bharat Bhojan records established cultural baselines rather than prescribing an exclusive recipe.
  • No Medical or Dietary Advice: All content is documented purely for cultural, historical, and computer science educational purposes. The platform makes zero therapeutic, medical, or clinical health claims.
